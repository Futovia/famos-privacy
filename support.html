What the app is

famOS is a private app for one family: a shared schedule, activities, to-dos and chores, bills and who owes whom, shared and shopping lists, notes, announcements, documents, and a Time Capsule of photos, voice recordings and text that are encrypted on your phone before upload. iPhone only. Members join by an invitation code from the family owner.

Get help

  • Email: joao@futovia.com
  • Include: the screen you were on, what you tapped, what you expected, what happened. Do not send passwords, passphrases or recovery codes.
  • Lost password: Sign in → Forgot password? → enter your email and recovery code, then choose a new password. A new recovery code is shown once; write it down. Signed in with Apple: "Forgot vault passphrase?" on the unlock screen works the same way.
  • Without the recovery code, encrypted memories cannot be opened by anyone, including us. Accounts created before 3 October 2026 have no recovery code and cannot reset a forgotten password or passphrase.

Sign in

  • Sign in with Apple, or email and password. Google sign-in is not available.
  • Apple sign-in asks for a vault passphrase on first sign-up. It encrypts your memories, never leaves your phone, and cannot be reset by us.

Delete your account

  1. Settings → Delete account.
  2. Enter your password (or vault passphrase if you signed in with Apple).
  3. If you own a family that still has other members, transfer ownership first: Settings → Members → choose a member → Transfer ownership.

What happens: your sign-in, name, email, keys, push tokens and contact addresses are removed. Posts you shared stay with your family under "Deleted member". Details: privacy policy, Retention and deletion.

Stop a shared list link

  1. Open the list (To Do → Lists, or from the event's Shared list).
  2. Tap Stop sharing. The web page shows "Not found." from then on.
  3. Links also expire 30 days after they were made. Send again to make a new one.

Common questions

  • Email and Text buttons: they open your Mail or Messages app with the text filled in. Nothing is sent by our server.
  • Venmo: Pay opens the Venmo app or website with the recipient; approval or "I paid" in famOS does not move money.
  • Add to Calendar: adds the event to the calendar on your phone. Nothing leaves the phone.
  • Notifications: allow them in iPhone Settings → famOS. Reminders arrive an hour before an event by default; change under Advanced on the event.
  • Sections missing: Settings → Show in app. Basic hides Activities, Money, Time Capsule, Announcements, Chores and Shopping lists; Standard or Everything brings them back.
  • Teens: a teen's posts are visible to their linked parent and may wait for approval; teens cannot assign to-dos to others or request money from anyone but their parent.
